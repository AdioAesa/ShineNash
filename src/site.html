Good questions

Everything people ask before booking.

Are your products safe for kids and pets?
Yes. We clean with Green Seal and EPA Safer Choice certified products — effective, non-toxic, and scented with cedar and citrus instead of bleach. If anyone in your home has sensitivities, tell us and we’ll go fragrance-free.
Do I need to be home during the clean?
Nope — most clients aren’t. Share a door code, lockbox, or garage pin through your booking and we’ll lock up behind us. You’ll get a text with a photo recap the moment your crew finishes.
Will I really get the same team every time?
Yes — that’s the whole point. Your home is assigned a dedicated trio who learn it visit by visit. If someone’s out sick, a trained floater joins the two people who already know your house, never a brand-new crew.
Are you insured and bonded?
Fully. ShineNash carries $2M in general liability coverage, every cleaner is bonded and background-checked, and our crews are W-2 employees — not app contractors. Certificates of insurance available on request for commercial clients and building managers.
What if I’m not happy with a clean?
Tell us within 24 hours and the Encore Guarantee kicks in: your crew comes back and re-cleans the area free. No forms, no debate. If we ever miss twice, that visit is on us.
Do you bring your own supplies and equipment?
Always — including HEPA-filter vacuums, microfiber systems, and all products. Prefer we use something of yours (a specific floor cleaner, for example)? Just leave it out and let us know.
How fast can you turn over an Airbnb?
Same day, between check-out and check-in. We handle linens, restocking, staging to your listing photos, and send a damage/condition report with photos after every turn — so you know your unit is guest-ready without driving over.
What’s your cancellation policy?
Life happens. Reschedule or cancel free up to 24 hours before your visit. Inside 24 hours there’s a $50 fee — because your crew’s day was planned around you.
