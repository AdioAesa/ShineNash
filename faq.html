Questions, answered.

Don't see yours? Text us at (615) 290-1866 — a real person replies.

Do I need to be home during the cleaning?

No. Most clients give us a door code, lockbox, or leave a key with a neighbor. If you'd rather be home the first time, that's completely fine too.

Do you bring your own supplies and equipment?

Yes — everything, including HEPA-filter vacuums and plant-based, fragrance-light products. If you have a favorite product or a sensitivity, tell us and we'll use what you prefer.

How do you price a cleaning?

By the size and condition of your home, not by the hour. You'll get a flat quote before we arrive, and that's the number you pay. Recurring plans get a discount off the standard rate.

What if I'm not happy with something?

Tell us within 24 hours and we'll come back and re-clean that area at no charge. We'd rather fix it than have you live with it.

Are your cleaners insured and background-checked?

Every ShineNova cleaner is a W-2 employee (not a contractor), background-checked, and covered by our liability insurance and workers' comp.

Can I get the same cleaner each time?

For recurring plans, yes — we assign you a lead cleaner and keep the same team whenever possible so they learn your home.

How far ahead do I need to book?

Same-week openings are usually available. Move-out and deep cleans book fastest at the end of the month, so a little notice helps.

Do you clean Airbnbs or offices?

Not yet — we focus on homes so we can do them really well. Ask us anyway; we may be able to recommend someone.

Ready to come home to shine?

Book in under a minute. Same-week openings across Nashville, and a happiness guarantee on every visit.
